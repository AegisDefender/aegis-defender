# Security reporting

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately to **ctrig@trigwebdesign.com** with the subject **Aegis security report**. Do not include credentials, private keys, customer logs or exploit details in public issues or pull requests.

Include the affected product and version, deployment context, a concise impact description, and reproduction steps from an environment you are authorized to test. Use synthetic data and sanitized evidence. Contact us first to arrange an appropriate transfer method for sensitive material.

This repository contains public documentation, not product source or an executable release. Contact Aegis to confirm supported commercial versions and update guidance.

This policy does not authorize testing of Aegis infrastructure, customers or third-party systems, and does not establish a bounty program or response-time commitment.
